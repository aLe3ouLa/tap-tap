Add Tab and TabGroup components. TabGroup supports a `line` (underlined) and `card` (bordered chip) variant, `default`/`small` sizes, `left`/`center` alignment, and an optional bottom border; individual Tabs support disabled and closable (×) states and an icon-start slot, with two-way `selectedIndex` binding on the group.
